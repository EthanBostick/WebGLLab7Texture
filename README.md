## Reflection Questions

What is the purpose of a color map?
A color (albedo/diffuse) map provides the base visible colors and surface patterns of a 3D object, such as wood grain or brick patterns, without any pre-baked lighting or shadows.

What does a normal map simulate?
A normal map simulates small surface bumps, grooves, and dents by altering how light bounces off each pixel, creating the illusion of 3D surface depth without adding extra polygons[cite: 1].

How does a roughness map affect lighting?
A roughness map controls how shiny or matte different parts of a surface look by determining whether incoming light reflects sharply (smooth/glossy) or scatters softly (rough/matte)[cite: 1].

Which texture improved realism the most and why?
The hardwood floor texture improved realism the most because the floor takes up the largest visual area in the scene and directly reflects the overhead point light and flickering fireplace light. Replacing the flat brown plane with wood planks, subtle normal grooves, and realistic roughness immediately grounded the room[cite: 1].

What advantages do texture maps have over adding more geometry?
Texture maps create high visual detail with a low polygon count, allowing scenes to load faster, use less memory, and maintain high frame rates compared to modeling every individual brick or wood grain with actual 3D vertices[cite: 1].
